About

Welcome! I’m Jiqi Gu, a PhD researcher in ecology working at the intersection of community ecology, functional traits, and eco-evolutionary modelling. My research combines bryophyte field studies, biodiversity databases, and quantitative models to understand how ecological processes shape biodiversity across space and time.
