Outreach

I share ecological research and quantitative methods through science communication, university teaching, open tutorials and invited lectures. My aim is to make ecological ideas and analytical workflows easier to understand and apply, from the logic of community assembly to practical modelling with HMSC, joint species distribution models and GEN3SIS.

This work has involved public-facing WeChat channels, graduate-level teaching at Northeast Agricultural University, online public courses associated with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and invited sessions hosted by universities and research-learning communities. Science communication

I founded Community Ecology, a WeChat Official Account for sharing community-ecology research and methods. Its editorial work includes accessible discussions of influential ecological papers and concepts, helping readers connect ecological theory with current research questions and analytical practice.

I also serve as a resident editor for Ecology Document Sharing, a public-facing WeChat Official Account that circulates research articles and educational content. The account’s articles have received approximately 160,000 reads. Across both channels, I work to present research in a form that is useful to students, researchers and readers outside a specialist audience.

Teaching and methods tutorials

Graduate teaching · Northeast Agricultural University

In 2023, I taught Advanced Experimental Design as an instructor for a PhD course at Northeast Agricultural University. The course connected study design with ecological data analysis: topics included field sampling, experimental design, ordination, structural equation modelling, species distribution models, joint species distribution models and mixed-effects models. The teaching emphasized how to match an analytical method to a research question and how to interpret model results in an ecological context.

Online public courses · Chinese Academy of Sciences

In 2024 and 2026, I taught the online public course Joint Species Distribution Modelling through the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ online course programme. Each edition reached more than 7,000 participants. The course introduces the rationale of modelling multiple species jointly and demonstrates how community data can be analysed alongside environmental covariates, functional traits, phylogenetic relationships and spatial structure. It also covers model fitting, checking, interpretation and ecological applications.

In addition to the courses, tutorials on HMSC, GEN3SIS and community ecology have received approximately 96,000 cumulative reads. My public HMSC learning materials include a step-by-step tutorial and reproducible R code, using bryophyte community data to demonstrate model setup and interpretation.
