Research

I study how environmental filtering, functional traits, dispersal and evolutionary history shape biodiversity across space and time. My work combines field ecology, global databases and quantitative models, from bryophyte community assembly to historical biogeography and multi-taxon community dynamics.

Trait-based biodiversity and community assembly

I am developing WorldBryDat, a global bryophyte functional-trait database covering 6,123 species and approximately 264 traits: 195 morphological, 12 reproductive, 49 life-history and 8 habitat preference traits [31]. It provides a basis for comparing bryophyte ecological strategies across environments and lineages.

Tibetan bryophyte sampling

My research on Tibetan bryophytes combines field surveys (551 plots, 1,008 species and 264 traits), functional traits, phylogeny and HMSC to examine environmental filtering and community assembly [28]. Related work investigates bryophyte distributions, habitat associations and intraspecific trait variation; bryophyte taxonomy and records; and plant community diversity and phylogenetic structure.

Eco-evolutionary modelling and historical biogeography

I am developing HmscEE, a spatially explicit framework that extends joint species distribution modelling to study biodiversity through deep time [30]. It combines species occurrences, functional traits, phylogenies, fossil evidence, palaeoenvironmental reconstructions, continental movement and dispersal opportunities. Its bryophyte case study uses approximately 4.4 million occurrence records across 515 million years.

Global biodiversity dynamics and species distribution modelling

Using LIFEPLAN observations, I examine how urbanization relates to community turnover, recurrence and temporal stability across 3,426 bird species and 462,956 molecular taxa of arthropods and fungi [27]. I have contributed to studies of marine fish distributions using expert maps and occurrence records [26, 22], and to evaluations of species distribution model performance and cross-validation [25, 20].

Earlier empirical and applied ecology

My earlier research spans bryophyte ecology and neural-network methods [6], soil nitrogen responses to freeze–thaw cycles [5], and water-quality assessment, ecological risk monitoring and related applications of artificial intelligence [8, 4, 3, 2, 1].
